adopt --project P [--forge F] --owner O --name N --landing B [--role owned|fork|context] [--prefix X] [--no-home] [--no-ci] [--check G]... --key NAME
  --project P: a project's name or number; required. The project
  --forge F: a number from 0 to 65535; optional. The forge's number; 1 when absent
  --owner O: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; required. The repository's owner on the forge
  --name N: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; required. The repository's name
  --landing B: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; required. The branch its changes land on
  --role ROLE: one of owned, fork or context; optional. owned, fork or context; owned when absent
  --prefix X: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. The prefix of temper's branches; temper/ when absent
  --no-home: a switch. Do not make it the project's home
  --no-ci: a switch. Do not use the forge's CI
  --check G: a number; repeated, at most 8. A gate, by its number, each once
Adopt a forge's repository into a project
Prints: `Adopted OWNER/NAME on forge F as ROLE.`, whether it is the project's home, and what temper may do there
--json: the record adopt.
Key: --key NAME, required.
A project has one home; a context repository is read, never
written, and never the home. A refusal says what mends it: another
project writes the repository (adopt it as context there), the
project has a home already (add --no-home, or choose a new
project), or the branch prefix is another deployment's (choose an
unused one); the last two print the call to make, with a new key.
`It is not known whether OWNER/NAME was adopted.` (exit 1): make
the same call again, with the same key.
