amend N [--rev R] [--priority P | --untracked] [--deadline T | --no-deadline] [--instructions FILE] [--spec FILE] [--dependency M]... [--no-dependencies] [--model M [--effort E]] REASON [--key NAME]
  N (task): a number; required. Task number
  --rev R: a number; optional. Shown spec revision; none for a tracked task's priority alone
  --priority P: a number; optional. Track the task at this priority
  --untracked: a switch. Stop tracking the task
  --deadline T: text, at most 1048576 bytes; optional. New deadline, as 2026-10-24T17:00Z
  --no-deadline: a switch. Remove the deadline
  --instructions FILE: a file, at most 1048576 bytes; optional. File of the whole instructions, or -; empty for none
  --spec FILE: a file, at most 1048576 bytes; optional. File of the spec's words, or -
  --dependency M: a number; repeated, at most 32. A dependency; repeated, the whole list
  --no-dependencies: a switch. Remove every dependency
  --model M: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Configured model name
  --effort E: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Effort offered by that model
  REASON (reason): text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; required. Reason for the amendment
Amend a task: each change named, the rest kept
--json: the record amend.
Key: --key NAME, optional.
