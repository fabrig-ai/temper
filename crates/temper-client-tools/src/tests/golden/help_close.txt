close N [--move D]... [--reason TEXT] --key NAME
  N (task): a number; required. Task number
  --move D: a number; repeated, at most 8. Delegate to move to the party
  --reason TEXT: text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Reason, empty by default
Close a chat and cancel or move its delegates
--json: the record close.
Key: --key NAME, required.
