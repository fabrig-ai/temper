decide tN/K|pN/K accept|reject|pass-up [REASON] [--running stays|moves] [--key NAME]
  P/K (proposal): a proposal, tN/K or pN/K; required. Typed proposer and proposal number
  OUTCOME (outcome): one of accept, reject or pass-up; required. Decision
  REASON (reason): text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Reason
  --running stays|moves: one of stays or moves; optional. Running work stays by default
decide N --hold R release|leave|pass-up [REASON] [--key NAME]
  N (task): a number; required. Task number
  --hold R: a number; required. Hold revision
  OUTCOME (outcome): one of release, leave or pass-up; required. Decision
  REASON (reason): text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Reason
Decide a proposal or an escalation
--json: the record decide.
Key: --key NAME, optional.
