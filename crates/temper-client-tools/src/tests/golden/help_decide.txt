decide tN/K|pN/K accept|reject|pass-up [REASON] [--running stays|moves] [--key NAME]
  P/K (proposal): a proposal, tN/K or pN/K; required. The proposal, as the inbox prints it
  OUTCOME (outcome): one of accept, reject or pass-up; required. The decision
  REASON (reason): text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Why; sent with reject alone
  --running stays|moves: one of stays or moves; optional. For a settings proposal: running work stays on its revision, or moves to the new one; stays when absent
decide N --hold R release|leave|pass-up [REASON] [--key NAME]
  N (task): a number; required. The held task's number
  --hold R: a number; required. The hold's revision, as the task prints it
  OUTCOME (outcome): one of release, leave or pass-up; required. The decision
  REASON (reason): text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Why; sent with leave alone
Decide a proposal, or the escalation of a held task
Prints: `done: decide`, or `decided first:`, by whom, how and when, if it was decided already
--json: the record decide.
Key: --key NAME, optional.
A decision made already, by your party or another, answers
`decided first:` with exit 0 and changes nothing.
