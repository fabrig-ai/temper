landing N [--project P]
  N (task): a number from 1 to 18446744073709551615; required. The landing task's number
  --project P: a project's name or number; optional. Its project; the task's when absent
Read a landing: its members, their candidates, each gate's proof and the turn
Prints: `landing N`, its project and phase, each member's change, pull request, candidate and gates, and the turn
--json: the record landing.
Key: none.
A landing lands its changes by turn: each gate is proved by a receipt
of its key, then main is fast-forwarded to each candidate. A task with
no landing fails, `no landing for task N` (exit 2).
