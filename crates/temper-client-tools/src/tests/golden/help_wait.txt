wait SUBJECT [--since DIGEST] [--until CONDITION] [--timeout DURATION]
  SUBJECT (subject): a subject; required. What to watch, from the list below
  --since DIGEST: a digest, as a read prints it; optional. The digest of your last read of the subject
  --until CONDITION: a condition; optional. A condition of the subject, from the list below
  --timeout DURATION: a duration: seconds, or a number with s, m or h, at most 3600 seconds; optional. How long to wait; 300 seconds when absent
wait forges [--since DIGEST] [--until settled|connected:F|made:F] [--timeout DURATION]
  forges (subject): one of forges; required. The deployment's forges
  --since DIGEST: a digest, as a read prints it; optional. The digest of your last read of forges
  --until settled|connected:F|made:F: one of settled, connected:N or made:N with N from 1 to 65535; optional. settled: none setting up or connecting; connected:F: forge F connected; made:F: forge F's first repository made
  --timeout DURATION: a duration: seconds, or a number with s, m or h, at most 3600 seconds; optional. How long to wait; 300 seconds when absent
wait change N [--project P] [--since DIGEST] [--until pull|ci:pending|ci:passed|ci:failed|held|landed] [--timeout DURATION]
  change (subject): one of change; required. A change's way to landing
  N (task): a number from 1 to 18446744073709551615; required. The change task's number
  --project P: a project's name or number; optional. Its project; the task's when absent
  --since DIGEST: a digest, as a read prints it; optional. The digest of your last read of the change
  --until pull|ci:pending|ci:passed|ci:failed|held|landed: one of pull, ci:pending, ci:passed, ci:failed, held or landed; optional. pull: its pull request opened; ci:STATE: CI at its head in that state; held: a hold on it; landed: merged
  --timeout DURATION: a duration: seconds, or a number with s, m or h, at most 3600 seconds; optional. How long to wait; 300 seconds when absent
Wait for a subject to change or to meet a condition
Prints: The subject as its read prints it, then `met`, or `not met: timeout` or `not met: ended` with exit 3
--json: the record wait.
Key: none.
With `--since`, it waits for the subject to differ from that read;
with `--until`, for the condition; with both, for both; with
neither, for the subject's next change.
Subjects and their conditions:
  me: waiting
  inbox: waiting, updates
  task N: ended, waiting-on-me, held, replied, phase:PHASE
  tree N, run N: ended
  accounts: usable, settled
  tasks and its read's arguments, but --after and --ended: none
  forges, change N: as their forms say
ended covers done, failed and cancelled; phase: names a live phase.
waiting-on-me covers the task's tree. replied holds once the task
waits on someone, holds, closes or ends. settled counts a failed
connection as settled.
Live phases: waiting|idle|due|preparing|claimed|running|backing-off|active|closing|held
