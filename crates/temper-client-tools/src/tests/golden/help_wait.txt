wait SUBJECT [--since DIGEST] [--until CONDITION] [--timeout DURATION]
  SUBJECT (subject): a subject; required. Watch subject
  --since DIGEST: a digest, as a read prints it; optional. Wait for a different digest
  --until CONDITION: a condition; optional. Wait for a condition
  --timeout DURATION: a duration: seconds, or a number with s, m or h, at most 3600 seconds; optional. Timeout, 300 seconds by default
wait forges [--since DIGEST] [--until settled|connected:F|made:F] [--timeout DURATION]
  forges (subject): one of forges; required. The deployment's forges
  --since DIGEST: a digest, as a read prints it; optional. Wait past this digest
  --until settled|connected:F|made:F: one of settled, connected:N or made:N with N from 1 to 65535; optional. settled: none setting up or connecting; connected:F: forge F connected; made:F: first repository made
  --timeout DURATION: a duration: seconds, or a number with s, m or h, at most 3600 seconds; optional. At most 3600 seconds; default 300 seconds
wait change N [--project P] [--since DIGEST] [--until pull|ci:pending|ci:passed|ci:failed|held|landed] [--timeout DURATION]
  change (subject): one of change; required. A change's panel
  N (task): a number from 1 to 18446744073709551615; required. The change task's number
  --project P: a project's name or number; optional. Its project; default the task's
  --since DIGEST: a digest, as a read prints it; optional. Wait past this digest
  --until pull|ci:pending|ci:passed|ci:failed|held|landed: one of pull, ci:pending, ci:passed, ci:failed, held or landed; optional. The panel's condition
  --timeout DURATION: a duration: seconds, or a number with s, m or h, at most 3600 seconds; optional. At most 3600 seconds; default 300 seconds
Wait for a subject's change or condition
--json: the record wait.
Key: none.
Subjects and --until conditions:
  me: waiting
  inbox: waiting|updates (no subject flags)
  task N: ended|waiting-on-me|held|replied|phase:<phase>
  tree N: ended
  run N: ended
  accounts: usable|settled
  tasks: no condition; its read arguments except --after and --ended
Live phases: waiting|idle|due|preparing|claimed|running|backing-off|active|closing|held
Use --until ended for done, failed, cancelled or ended; terminal phase conditions are usage errors.
waiting-on-me covers the task's tree. An ended task answers with its ended read.
--since is the matching read's digest of the watched document, not its cursor.
--since and --until together require both change and condition.
Timeout: 300 seconds by default, at most 3600 seconds.
