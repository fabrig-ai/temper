# v0.1.0

Draft, 2026-10-26, from a session with the user; updated 2026-10-27
(the process model, the reference case's second edition, documentation
for agents). How temper gets to
v0.1.0: the bar it must pass, how that bar is climbed, the parts it
needs, and their order. Each part that is product has a draft of its
own, which this one points to; this one holds only the path. Nothing
here is adopted.

## 1. In one page

- **v0.1.0 is temper's first public release:** a person downloads one
  binary, runs `temper box` with their model, ChatGPT or Claude, and
  their forge, a Forgejo the box owns or GitHub, tells temper how they
  work, and temper runs that work. Its aim is the reference case's
  second edition (`reference-case.md`): temper's own development run
  on temper, by the process model (`process-model.md`). It may land a little
  narrower, close to it, with the rest in v0.2.0.
- **Its bar is a ladder of stories on real backends** (section 2),
  from the playground's requests up to the reference case, each walked
  through `temper ctl`: ChatGPT with an owned Forgejo first, then
  GitHub and Claude. Real backends lead; fakes hold what the real runs
  find.
- **A rung is climbed as a focused QA story** (section 3): a brief a QA
  session walks, a report of what it found, an owner who fixes it, and
  the same brief, rerun unchanged, as the acceptance.
- **QA after landing resumes, on real backends** (section 3).
- **The director triages every finding** (section 4): one on v0.1.0's
  happy path, the ladder, goes to the owner of what it names, fixed with
  a test in the lowest world that reproduces it; the rest waits in the
  backlog.
- **The parts** (section 5) each have their design elsewhere: the
  process model, configuring, upgrades, the playground in the browser,
  public reading, documentation for agents, the UI, the site.
- **Left out** (section 6): the terminal client, models and forges
  beyond the four, our own development on dev.fabrig.ai, TLS between
  hosts, hearth.
- **The reference case's second edition** (`reference-case.md`,
  10-27) is the top rung's story, frozen for v0.1.0 (section 7).
- **The order** is section 8; what done means, section 9.

### 2.2 The backends

- **The first pair is ChatGPT with an owned Forgejo:** every rung
  climbs on it first.
- **GitHub and Claude follow a rung behind.** A rung that passes on the
  first pair is walked again with GitHub for the forge, connected and
  then managed (`github.md`), and with Claude for the model, Claude
  Code on a person's own login (`../protocol/llm.md`). Each walk's
  failures are findings like any other.
- **C runs with both plans,** Claude's and ChatGPT's, as the case has
  them.
- **One holder per login:** a subscription's login refreshes, so it has
  one holder at a time, moved into each run's box and exported back
  before the box stops. Live runs on one login go one at a time.

## 3. Climbing a rung

- **A brief per rung,** in the shape of the focused QA stories of
  10-26: the scenario, the backends, the held login's move in and out,
  and a report with a verdict, the walk with its times, how hard the
  tool was to drive, the tooling it lacked, ranked, and each finding
  with its reproduction from a fresh box.
- **A QA session walks it,** as agents' `roles/qa.md` and `../qa.md`
  say, changing nothing but its scratch.
- **An owner makes it pass:** a mission per rung, or per two small
  ones, whose end is the same brief, rerun unchanged, passing. It fixes
  what the story needs, or hands a finding to the owner of what it
  names (section 4).
- **A rerun that passes is the rung's pass,** in one sitting, without
  the lab's verdicts. A rung that fails after passing has a finding,
  never a retry.
- **Then it holds:** the passing story becomes a live-tier story, as
  the owned-forge landing did (`../domain/testing.md`, 2.7), run before
  the release and whenever a change touches it; its fake twin goes into
  the worlds.
- **QA after landing resumes,** its passes held since their pilot, and
  climbs `../qa.md`'s rungs (its section 3) to real backends now: fakes,
  a real model, GitHub. Its passes take the held login in turn with the
  ladder's runs, one live run at a time. If that saturates the login,
  the director says so to the user.

## 4. Findings

- **Each names its reproduction** from a fresh box, what was expected
  and what was seen.
- **v0.1.0's happy path is the ladder:** its stories, and each part's
  bar in section 5, used as intended, on the four backends. A security
  hole, lost data and a flake that fails a gate count as on it. It
  replaces "the roadmaps' stories and pages" of agents'
  `roles/director.md`, 6.
- **The director triages every finding,** QA after landing's and any
  other, at its check (agents' `docs/design/qa-flow.md`, 6): one on the
  happy path goes at once to the owner of what it names, or its
  designer; any other goes to the backlog, where it waits until
  v0.1.0 is out.
- **A rung's own walk reports to its owner,** whose mission is to make
  that rung pass: its findings are on the path by definition, and the
  owner fixes them, or hands one through the director to the owner of
  what it names.
- **Its fix carries a test in the lowest world that reproduces it,**
  with the real run's transcript as a fake model's script where the
  model's turn matters (`qa-live.md`, 8); then the rung is walked again.
- **The tool's friction counts:** where the QA session stalled, guessed
  or read source to get on is a finding against `temper ctl` or the
  guide, since people's agents drive temper the same way.

## 7. The reference case, second edition

- **`reference-case.md`** (10-27, from a session with the user)
  applies `process-model.md` to an invented process like the one
  temper's own development runs: six acts, 20 steps, about nine hours,
  two repositories (kit and app), one owned Forgejo, both plans. It
  replaces the first edition as the top rung's story.
- **`porting.md`** maps our real process onto the case and orders its
  move onto temper. It names temper's own process, so the ladder cites
  the case, never the porting guide.
- **On real backends:** both plans, as the case has them; the workers
  on one machine; real time, where fakes scale it down. The person's
  words are a script a QA driver types through `temper ctl`.
- **Frozen for v0.1.0:** the process keeps changing, and a moving top
  rung would never pass. Later changes wait for the next edition.
- **The aim is the whole case.** v0.1.0 may stop a little short of it,
  leaving to v0.2.0 the acts whose gaps are not closed in time; which,
  is decided as C climbs.
